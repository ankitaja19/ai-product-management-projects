# AI × Product Management — From Ideas to Working Products

> A growing collection of hands-on projects where I explore how AI, vibe coding, and product thinking can turn everyday problems into working applications.

I'm a Product Manager who enjoys exploring new ways to build and solve problems. This repository is my space to experiment with AI-powered development tools, build practical PM tools, and share what I learn along the way.

The idea is simple: **Don't just think about products. Build them, test them, and learn from them.**

## Why this repository?

As a Product Manager, I've spent years working with engineering teams to bring products to life. With AI-powered coding tools, I'm exploring how much further I can take an idea myself — from identifying a problem to building and testing a working prototype.

Through this repository, I want to:

- Build tools that solve everyday Product Management problems.
- Experiment with vibe coding and AI-assisted development.
- Understand the technology behind the products I build.
- Share working demos, experiments, and learnings.
